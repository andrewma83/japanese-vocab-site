Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

In short: Japanese Vocab keeps your word list in your own Google Drive and on your iPhone. We do not run a server, receive your words, show ads, track you, or sell your data.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Japanese Vocab iPhone app and its Lock Screen widget (“the app”). In this policy, “we” means the developer of the app.

Information the app accesses

  • Google account profile. When you sign in, Google provides your name, email address, and profile photo to the app. The app uses your email address to show which account is connected. It does not use your profile photo.
  • Your word sheet. With the Google Drive drive.file permission, the app can see, edit, create, and delete only the Drive files you use with this app. In practice, this is the Japanese Vocab spreadsheet it creates. It holds the words, readings, meanings, JLPT levels, widget settings, and dates you add, plus a small settings tab.
  • Data stored on your iPhone. The app stores a copy of your words, words waiting to upload, your sheet’s ID and name, your last sync time, and setup status. Google’s sign-in software stores your sign-in tokens in the iOS Keychain.

The app does not request your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier.

How the app uses this information

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To create, format, read, and update your word sheet.
  • To show your words in the app and Lock Screen widget.
  • To keep words you add offline and upload them when you reconnect.

Google user data

Japanese Vocab’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We use Google user data only to provide the features described in this policy. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Because the app has no developer-run server, Google user data moves only between your iPhone and Google.

How information is shared

We do not receive or share your words or Google account information. The app sends data over encrypted connections directly to Google so it can sign you in and sync your sheet. Google handles that data under the Google Privacy Policy.

The app uses Google’s sign-in software. Google may process account, device, approximate location, and usage information to provide sign-in, secure your account, and measure how the service performs. We do not receive this information.

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with crash and performance reports. Apple handles those reports under its own privacy policy.

Storage and retention

Your sheet stays in your Google Drive until you delete it. Data on your iPhone stays there until you remove it as described below. Signing out removes the saved Google sign-in, but keeps your cached words and any words waiting to upload so they are not lost.

Your choices

  • Sign out in the app’s Settings.
  • Remove the app’s Google access at Google Account permissions.
  • Delete your words by editing or deleting the Japanese Vocab spreadsheet in Google Drive.
  • Delete data on your iPhone by signing out and then deleting the app and its widget.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
