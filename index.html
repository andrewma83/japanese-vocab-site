One word, every hour.

Japanese Vocab is an iPhone app and Lock Screen widget that shows a new Japanese word each hour, from a list you keep in Google Sheets.

How it works

  1. 1
    Sign in with Google

    Connect the Google account where you want to keep your words.

  2. 2
    Create your word sheet

    The app adds a Japanese Vocab spreadsheet to your Google Drive.

  3. 3
    Add the widget

    Put it on your Lock Screen and see a new word every hour.

Your words stay yours

The app uses Google Sign-In and limited Google Drive access to create and sync the one spreadsheet that holds your word list. It cannot browse the other files in your Drive.

There are no ads, analytics, or developer-run servers. Your iPhone talks directly to Google. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
